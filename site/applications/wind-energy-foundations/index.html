01 / WIND TURBINE ANCHOR RODS

Foundation Anchor Bolts

Coordinate anchor-rod threads with the cage geometry.

Wind turbine foundation anchor bolts and prepared anchor-rod material are supplied for purchaser-designed foundation assemblies. Cyclic tension, maintained preload, thread-root geometry and transition details take priority over a static strength value alone.

Supply
Raw or heat-treated bar, thread-ready preparation, cut lengths and identity marking; complete cage design remains purchaser-defined.
Interfaces
Thread system, nuts and couplers, foundation template geometry, straightness, erection tolerance and protection zones.
Acceptance
Load spectrum, preload and fatigue requirements; heat identity, mechanical results, thread and dimensional records, and specified NDT.
Enquire About Foundation Anchor Bolts
02 / STRUCTURAL ANCHOR BARS

Holding-Down Bars

Align the embedded and exposed lengths.

Structural anchor bars and high-strength holding-down bars form part of the foundation anchorage range. Confirm the approved material, finished geometry and end interfaces for the project rather than treating an anchor-bar family as a completed foundation system.

Geometry
Overall length, embedment, projection, thread length and end transitions.
Connections
Nut and bearing interfaces, template fit, installation space and dimensional tolerances.
Order basis
Material and target properties, coating route, identification, tests and delivery quantities.
Enquire About Holding-Down Bars
03 / SHEAR KEY & CONNECTION BARS

Shear Connection Bars

Confirm the connection from its drawing.

Shear key bars and shear connection bars are available within structural anchor and foundation products. Supply follows the specified bar shape, dimensions, material and connection detail; the engineer defines the shear-transfer mechanism and acceptance requirements.

Scope
Shear key bars / shear connection bars to the purchaser’s drawing; no standard connection capacity is implied.
Geometry
Diameter or section, finished length, end preparation, embedment and installation interfaces.
Inputs
Design connection, material specification, tolerances, protection, inspection and quantity.
Enquire About Shear Connection Bars
04 / BOLTING & STUD MATERIAL

High-Strength Fastener Bar

Specify the installed pretension.

Alloy-steel bar routes support purchaser-specified bolting and stud manufacture. Property class, thread manufacture, nut pairing, coating friction and installation method must achieve the specified preload while controlling relaxation.

Supply
QT or peeled bar and machining allowance; fastener properties follow the full manufacturing and assembly specification.
Inputs
Property class, nut pairing, thread route, coating or lubricant condition and installation method.
Records
Delivery condition and mechanical results, thread-gauge records, identity marking and ordered finish checks.
Enquire About High-Strength Fastener Bar
05 / HEAVY ROTATING & ARTICULATED PARTS

Shaft & Pin Blanks

Check toughness through the controlling section.

Heat-treated, peeled or rough-machined shaft and pin blanks are supplied for heavy rotating or articulated parts. The controlling section, service temperature, impact requirement and internal-soundness acceptance govern the material route.

Supply
Forged or rolled route, QT condition, machining allowance and UT when specified.
Inputs
Finished geometry, heat-treatment section, minimum temperature, load duty, test location and NDT acceptance.
Records
Heat-treatment lot identity, section-relevant mechanical results, dimensional report and specified UT record.
Enquire About Shaft & Pin Blanks
06 / MATCHED THREADED COMPONENTS

Couplers, Nuts & Ends

Maintain full mating-thread engagement.

Drawing-defined couplers, nuts and prepared ends are supplied to agreed dimensions and acceptance criteria. Thread form, class, handedness, engagement length and protective finish must remain compatible across all mating components.

Supply
Machining, the agreed thread route and protective finish when specified.
Inputs
Thread standard, size, class, handedness, minimum engagement, mating geometry and finish allowance.
Records
Gauge plan, dimensional and engagement records, component marking and finish inspection.
Enquire About Couplers, Nuts & Ends
07 / WIND TOWERS & FOUNDATION CONNECTIONS

Post-Tensioning Bars

Post-tensioning for wind-energy concrete structures

We supply smooth, fully threaded and cold-rolled fully threaded steel bars with anchorage, installation, grouting and protection accessories for wind-energy concrete structures. Bar properties, connection details and stressing requirements follow the purchaser's approved design.

Bar types
Three separate bar routes: smooth bars, fully threaded PT bars and cold-rolled fully threaded bars.
Interfaces
Confirm threads, couplers, nuts, bearing plates, washers, anchorage-zone reinforcement and installation accessories together.
Order basis
For the cold-rolled route, confirm the actual strength class, material standard, dimensions and testing. A route name is not a finished-product performance rating.

SUPPLY SCOPE

A Clear Supply Scope.
A Better Project Brief.

JOTAIN supplies foundation anchor bars and bolts, structural connection bars, bolting material, processed fittings, shaft and pin blanks, and PT products against the purchaser’s project requirements.

Our scope
Confirmed bar and component supply, material condition, end preparation, dimensions, specified protection, inspection, traceability and delivery records.
Engineering team
Foundation and tower design, fatigue spectrum, target preload, member and connection capacities, template and erection geometry, installation method and final acceptance.
JOTAIN supplies materials and processed components to the purchaser’s drawings and specifications. Final system design remains the responsibility of the purchaser’s authorised engineering team.

Published bar sizes and reference material routes are not foundation-system capacities, fatigue-life ratings or thread-assembly performance. Supply is confirmed product by product.

01 / STEEL COMPONENTS

Loading & Preload

Thread-root fatigue needs explicit inputs

The purchaser’s fatigue and preload model defines the material review. Cyclic tension, maintained preload, thread roots and transition details must be considered beyond static strength alone.

Inputs
Design load spectrum, target preload, preload loss or relaxation criteria, thread-root detail and connection stiffness.
Checks
Material condition, thread manufacture, nut pairing, coating friction, installation method and ordered test results.
02 / STEEL COMPONENTS

Temperature & Protection

Match toughness and finish to service

Minimum service temperature and exposure affect impact toughness and protection. Section size and the heat-treatment route influence mechanical acceptance.

Inputs
Minimum service temperature, corrosion category, controlling section and impact-test requirements.
Process
Confirm coating-related hydrogen risk, post-process controls, protective finish and packing.
03 / STEEL COMPONENTS

Mating Interfaces

Coordinate threads, template geometry and erection tolerances

Thread, nut, coupler and foundation template geometry must be compatible. Finish allowance must retain minimum engagement and assembly fit.

Interfaces
Thread standard, size, class and handedness; nut and coupler engagement.
Acceptance
Straightness, finished length, template and erection tolerances, gauge plan and component identification.
