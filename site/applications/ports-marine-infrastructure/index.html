SUPPLY SCOPE

A Clear Supply Scope.
A Better Project Brief.

For quay-wall ties, marine anchorage, connection and adjustment components, and heavy machinery interfaces, JOTAIN confirms materials, processed components and accessories against the purchaser’s drawings and specifications.

Our scope
Product lists, materials and dimensions, threads and end processing, protection, inspection plans, marking and delivery records.
Engineering team
System design, design loads and fatigue assessment, exposure and design life, protection and cathodic interfaces, anchorage layout and final acceptance requirements.
JOTAIN supplies materials and processed components to the purchaser’s drawings and specifications. Final system design remains the responsibility of the purchaser’s authorised engineering team.

Supplying materials and processed components does not constitute design approval for a complete quay-wall, anchorage or marine tie system. Specifications, performance, protection and acceptance documents are confirmed for each product and project.
