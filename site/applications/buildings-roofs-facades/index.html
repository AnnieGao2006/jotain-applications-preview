01 / TENSION RODS & HANGERS

Architectural Tie Rods

Keep the visible load path aligned.

Architectural tension rods and hangers provide the bar and end-connection route for purchaser-designed roofs, facades, suspended members and long-span structures. Finished length, straightness, eccentricity, rod-end geometry and pin alignment must keep the tension path concentric.

Supply
Heat-treated bar, threads or prepared ends, and finish protection to the order.
Geometry
Design tension, preload and eccentricity; fork gap, pin diameter, hole fit and effective thread engagement.
Erection
Length tolerance, adjustment range, transport limits and installation sequence; visible finish and damage protection.
Enquire About Architectural Tie Rods
02 / ARCHITECTURAL COMPRESSION MEMBERS

Compression Struts

Coordinate the strut and its end connections.

Compression strut systems are offered for roofs, facades and long-span architectural structures. Confirm the member and end connections against the purchaser’s drawings, including effective length, restraint, connection geometry and the required finish. System sizing and stability verification remain with the project engineer.

Scope
Compression members and their drawing-defined end connections; supply form and manufacturing route are confirmed for each order.
Geometry
Member section, pin-to-pin length, end restraint, fork and pin geometry, tolerances and any permitted adjustment.
Inputs
Design compression, buckling requirements, material specification, inspection, finish and installation conditions.
Enquire About Compression Struts
03 / BUILDING & FOUNDATION ANCHORAGE

Anchor & Hold-Down Rods

Match setting-out to the erection sequence.

We supply bar and prepared threaded material for drawing-defined building anchorage. Projection, embedment, thread length, bearing plate and nut interfaces, and installation tolerance must match the foundation layout and erection sequence.

Supply
Specified bar, cut lengths, prepared threads, identity marking and protected packing.
Interfaces
Finished length and straightness, embedded and exposed lengths, plate holes, nut engagement and adjustment allowance.
Inputs
Structural drawing, material and property requirements, quantity, corrosion category and inspection basis.
Enquire About Anchor & Hold-Down Rods
04 / FORKS, SPADES & COUPLERS

Connection Fittings

Preserve concentric force transfer.

Machined forks, spades and couplers are matched to the approved rod and pin geometry. Pin diameter, hole fit, fork gap, spade thickness, coupler engagement and finish allowance must preserve the connection alignment.

Supply
Drawing-defined machining, dimensional inspection and protective finish when specified.
Interfaces
Rod-end threads, pin and hole fit, fork gap, spade thickness and minimum coupler engagement.
Acceptance
Approved tolerances, gauging and fit checks; inspect the visible finish and protect mating surfaces in transit.
Enquire About Connection Fittings
05 / TURNBUCKLES & THREADED BAR

Adjustment & Fastener Bar

Keep engagement throughout the adjustment.

We supply adjustment components and bar material for purchaser-specified connections. Left- and right-hand threads, usable adjustment, lock-off, engagement witness and erection sequence must be defined together.

Scope
Turnbuckles and fastener bar material, with the material route, thread compatibility and assembly checks agreed for the order.
Adjustment
Confirm minimum engagement at both adjustment limits, thread handedness, locking and finished-length tolerance.
Finish
Confirm coating or plating allowance, visible acceptance and protection during transport and installation.
Enquire About Adjustment & Fastener Bar
06 / BUILDINGS, LONG SPANS & PRECAST

Post-Tensioning Bars

Post-tensioning for building concrete and precast connections

We supply smooth, fully threaded and cold-rolled fully threaded steel bars with anchorage, installation, grouting and protection accessories for building concrete and precast connections. Bar properties, connection details and stressing requirements follow the purchaser's approved design.

Bar types
Three separate bar routes: smooth bars, fully threaded PT bars and cold-rolled fully threaded bars.
Interfaces
Confirm threads, couplers, nuts, bearing plates, washers, anchorage-zone reinforcement and installation accessories together.
Order basis
For the cold-rolled route, confirm the actual strength class, material standard, dimensions and testing. A route name is not a finished-product performance rating.
Enquire About PT Bars

SUPPLY SCOPE

A Clear Supply Scope.
A Better Project Brief.

JOTAIN supplies architectural tension and compression members, anchorage, connection and adjustment components, and PT products to the purchaser’s approved drawings and specifications.

Our scope
Material and product lists, finished dimensions, threads and end preparation, finish, inspection, marking and delivery records.
Engineering team
Member forces, tension and compression design, stability, eccentricity, connection geometry, erection sequence, fire-design and durability requirements.
JOTAIN supplies materials and processed components to the purchaser’s drawings and specifications. Final system design remains the responsibility of the purchaser’s authorized engineering team.

The bracing illustration shows a tension load path only: one diagonal carries a given load direction; the other engages under reversal. Their crossing is not a centre connection. Compression struts are separate products with project-specific design requirements.
