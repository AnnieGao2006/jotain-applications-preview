01 / HEAT-TREATED RODS FOR ROD MILLS

Grinding Rods

Specify the rod around the working mill.

JOTAIN supplies finished, heat-treated 65Mn grinding rods with straightening, fixed-length cutting, inspection, marking and export packing. Rod dimensions, heat-treatment response and the balance between wear resistance and toughness are reviewed together against the actual rod-mill duty. Alternative materials require a separate technical review.

Supply
Heat-treated, straightened rods cut to the ordered length, with agreed end condition, identification and packing. Confirm diameter, tolerance, usable length and the mill’s handling limits.
Hardness
Agree the target band, scale and test method, surface and internal test positions where specified, sampling and heat-treatment lot uniformity. Hardness alone does not establish service life.
Mill duty
Provide feed characteristics, mill size and operating conditions, current rod material and size, consumption in kg/t, and any breakage, bending, spalling or tangling history.
Acceptance
Confirm straightness measurement and sampling, flat crack-free ends, surface condition, inspection records and trial quantity. Compare trials over matched operating conditions before assessing repeatability.

02 / HEAVY ROTATING & ARTICULATED PARTS

Shaft & Pin Blanks

Review the core at the controlling section.

Shaft and pin blanks for heavy rotating and articulated machinery are supplied through the agreed bar or forging route. Diameter, heat-treatment response, cyclic load and impact duty determine the property review; the critical section must be represented by the agreed test location.

Supply
QT bar, peeled stock or rough-machined blanks with drawing-defined allowance.
Section
Confirm ruling diameter, core toughness, hardness profile, test position and the OEM property targets.
Inspection
Agree internal soundness, UT when specified, surface acceptance, straightness, end condition and dimensional reports.
Enquire About Shaft & Pin Blanks
03 / HIGH-LOAD THREADED COMPONENTS

Tie & Fastener Bar

Control the thread-root fatigue zone.

Heat-treated alloy bar and prepared threaded material are reviewed for high-load machinery ties and fasteners. Material properties, preload, engagement and the thread-manufacturing route must suit the purchaser’s peak and cyclic loading requirements.

Supply
Heat-treated bar and thread-ready preparation or agreed end machining, with specified mechanical acceptance.
Interfaces
Thread standard and class, engagement, preload, thread roots, transition geometry and surface condition.
Next stage
Straightness, end condition, machining allowance, surface acceptance and UT where specified.
Enquire About Tie & Fastener Bar
04 / HEAVY COMPONENTS TO DRAWING

Rough-Machined Parts

Check internal quality before finish machining.

Rough-machined heavy parts are prepared through a rolled or forged material route with agreed machining allowance. The OEM drawing defines the critical section, heat-treatment responsibility, datums and inspection stages before final machining.

Supply
Rolled or forged stock, rough machining and a drawing-specific dimensional report.
Before cutting
Resolve UT stage and acceptance, internal soundness and the heat-treatment condition at the critical section.
Allowance
Confirm datums, stock allowance, straightness and surface condition for the next machining or assembly operation.
Enquire About Rough-Machined Parts
05 / SOLID GFRP ROCK BOLTS

Solid FRP Bolts

Match a solid tendon to the excavation sequence.

Solid FRP rock bolts provide a cuttable rod route for face reinforcement and ground support where later excavation is planned. The solid GFRP tendon is installed in a pre-drilled hole with the project-specified grout or resin system.

Rod
Confirm solid cross-section, profile, diameter, length and required tensile and head-assembly properties.
Installation
Agree hole geometry, grout or resin compatibility, cure requirements and installation equipment.
Mating parts
Match plate and nut material to the required cuttability, bearing surface and project acceptance.
Enquire About Solid FRP Bolts
06 / HOLLOW GFRP ROCK BOLTS

Hollow FRP Bolts

Use the hollow core for the agreed grouting route.

Hollow FRP rock bolts combine a cuttable GFRP tendon with an internal passage for grout. This product route uses a pre-drilled hole; self-drilling bolts are reviewed separately for their drilling and connection requirements.

Rod
Confirm outer and inner diameters, length, profile and the required rod and head-assembly acceptance.
Grouting
Agree grout compatibility, injection connection, sealing and the specified installation procedure.
Mating parts
Confirm plate, nut and any extension interface, including the required cuttability and bearing arrangement.
Enquire About Hollow FRP Bolts
07 / SELF-DRILLING HOLLOW ROCK BOLTS

Self-Drilling Bolts

Coordinate the bar, drill bit and grout path.

A self-drilling hollow rock-bolt route combines a threaded hollow bar, drill bit and grout passage. The ground conditions and installation equipment govern selection, including where a pre-drilled hole cannot remain open for conventional insertion.

Bar
Confirm the specified steel or FRP route, outer and inner diameters, thread form and section lengths.
Interfaces
Review drill-bit type, coupling engagement, injection connection, bearing plate and nut against the installation method.
Acceptance
Specify ground conditions, grout procedure, required support properties, protection and installation or pull-test records.
Enquire About Self-Drilling Bolts
08 / EXPANDABLE FRICTION ROCK BOLTS

Expandable Bolts

Match expansion to the borehole and ground.

Expandable friction bolts use a formed steel tube that expands against the borehole wall when installed with high-pressure water. This route is reviewed for roof and wall support with hole dimensions, installation equipment and ground conditions specified together.

Tube
Confirm profile, finished length, end fittings, corrosion protection and the required acceptance.
Installation
Agree hole size and condition, expansion equipment and the supplier-approved installation procedure.
Verification
Specify installation records, inspection and project-required pull testing. Capacity is confirmed for the selected product and ground conditions.
Enquire About Expandable Bolts
09 / STEEL OR FRP ROCK-BOLT ROUTES

Combination Bolts

Specify the complete anchorage arrangement.

Combination rock bolts are reviewed as steel or FRP product configurations against the excavation sequence. Specify the intended initial anchorage and subsequent bonding or grouting arrangement, together with cuttability and protection requirements where applicable.

Configuration
State the selected steel or FRP route, rod, sleeve or pipe, anchorage head and any grout passage. These are alternative configurations, not an assumed steel–FRP hybrid.
Sequence
Confirm immediate-support requirements, later grouting, temporary or permanent duty and the approved installation method.
Interfaces
Review plate, nut, connection fit, corrosion protection and project-required system verification.
Enquire About Combination Bolts
10 / EXPANSION-HEAD ROCK ANCHORAGE

Mechanical Rock Bolts

Review the rod and expansion head together.

Mechanical rock-bolt assemblies combine a load-bearing rod with a matched expansion head. The accepted drawing and ground conditions govern the rod, head geometry, hole size and installation requirements.

Assembly
Confirm rod material and profile, expansion shell and cone, head connection, bearing plate and nut.
Installation
Specify borehole diameter and straightness, rock condition, tensioning method and the approved setting procedure.
Verification
Confirm engagement, installed load or testing requirements, corrosion protection and any subsequent grouting. Product ratings and installation acceptance are agreed for the selected assembly.
Enquire About Mechanical Rock Bolts
11 / MECHANICAL ANCHOR HEADS

Bail Anchors

Confirm the head and its mating hardware.

Bail-type mechanical anchors are reviewed with the specified rod and mating hardware. The setting arrangement, hole geometry and ground conditions control selection; the supplied head configuration must match the approved installation procedure.

Head
Confirm shell or wedge configuration, rod diameter, thread connection and the required load basis.
Hardware
Match the bearing plate, nut and washer arrangement to the head and rod.
Installation
Confirm the setting and tensioning method, grout-tube arrangement if specified, and project-required inspection or pull testing.
Enquire About Bail Anchors
12 / TUNNEL & HEADING SUPPORT

Lattice Girders

Match the frame to the excavation geometry.

Lattice girders are three-dimensional steel frames for tunnel and heading support. Main bars, secondary bars, stiffeners and connection details are reviewed against the purchaser’s support design and excavation profile.

Geometry
Confirm radius, frame section, segment lengths, spacing and erection tolerance.
Connections
Specify connection plates, footplates, joints and the interface with the surrounding support or lining.
Supply
Agree material, fabrication and weld requirements, dimensional checks, marking and delivery sequence.

Pipes
Confirm diameter, wall thickness, section length, steel specification and connection details.
Layout
Specify installation angle, pipe spacing, overlap, canopy length and drilling equipment interfaces.
Acceptance
Agree joint fit, straightness, grouting details where specified, corrosion protection and inspection records.

Grinding rods, machine parts and ground-support products have different acceptance requirements.

01 / STEEL COMPONENTS

Wear & Impact

Review damage alongside the hardness result

For grinding rods, abrasive wear, impact severity, breakage and spalling history must be considered together. A hardness reading at one location does not explain the complete rod or predict mill performance.

Mill inputs
Feed, mill duty, current rod material and dimensions, consumption, breakage, bending and replacement practice.
Acceptance
Target hardness and profile, heat-treatment consistency, toughness, straightness and end condition.
02 / STEEL COMPONENTS

Section & Fatigue

Represent the critical section and thread root

For shafts, pins and heavy parts, diameter and geometry affect heat-treatment response and core properties. Threaded machine parts additionally require preload, engagement and thread-root review.

Properties
Section hardenability, core toughness, hardness profile, cyclic and impact demand.
Interfaces
Thread manufacture, transition geometry, surface condition, machining allowance and assembly fit.

Manufacturing & Ground Support

Machinery

Support

Grinding-Rod & Machinery Order Basis

Finished, heat-treated 65Mn is the primary grinding-rod supply route. 40Cr and 42CrMoA enter alternative material review; no default hardness band or finished diameter range applies. Any alternative requires confirmed chemistry, heat treatment, acceptance and trial controls.

For general industrial machinery, 40Cr remains a candidate heat-treated or machined-blank route according to load, section, fatigue and machining requirements. For every bar route, specify the product standard, diameter, delivery condition, test positions, sampling and required records.

FRP and ground-support products require their own finished-product specifications, material identification, dimensions, head and connection requirements, installation procedure and project acceptance. The commercial steel-bar ranges above do not apply to them.
