SUPPLY SCOPE

A Clear Supply Scope.
A Better Project Brief.

JOTAIN coordinates bars, accessories and related products for post-tensioning, structural connections and tunnel support against the project procurement list.

Our scope
Product specification, material and dimensions, accessory interfaces, protection, inspection basis, identification and delivery records.
Engineering team
System design, load and fatigue calculations, stressing parameters, support layout and final engineering acceptance requirements.
JOTAIN supplies materials and processed components to the purchaser’s drawings and specifications. Final system design remains the responsibility of the purchaser’s authorised engineering team.

Bridge bearings, expansion joints, rails, wheelsets and vehicles are outside this page's product list. Complete bridge systems are not supplied. Dimensions, performance classes and certification documents require separate confirmation by product and project.
